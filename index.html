    Topic dictionary

    Each topic has ask words (what you type in the search box) and match patterns (what to look for inside messages). Plain words match from the start of a word, so refun catches refund, refunded and refunds. Wrap a pattern in slashes for a regular expression, e.g. /\btest\b/. Hinglish works fine, just add the spellings people actually use.

    Match columns

    Tell the analyzer which column holds what.

    AI question sorting

    OpenRouter sorts every question into an exact type, merges repeats into top questions and writes takeaways. Only question text is sent: no names, and phone numbers and emails are masked. Labels are cached in this browser, so the same question is never paid for twice.

    Make a separate key at openrouter.ai/keys and give it a small credit limit.
    A full sort of a two-session chat fits in one day. Free models can be slower or busy at peak hours, and their providers may log prompts.
